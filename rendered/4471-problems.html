How the reports split

  • parser. Every tenant in #4471 writes the day first, and the shared parser reads month first, so 7 April becomes 4 July. This is the problem the queued fix is for.
  • hint. The booking-v3 form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field, so people type month first. The form then reads the date day first. The result looks the same as the parser bug, but only on the three booking-v3 tenants, and only through the form.

A few parser reports describe a date that moves to the day before, not a swap, for example Tuesday 12 May shown as Monday 11 May. The desk keeps them under parser, by tenant, and lists them in desk-notes.json. If more come in, they may need a ticket of their own.

Merging on subject line put both causes in one ticket, because people use the same words for each. Group by what a report shows: its tenant, its channel, and its dates.

What to check before closing #4471

  1. The parser fix ships, and new parser reports stop, through every channel.
  2. The booking-v3 hint and the form agree on one order.
  3. The one-day moves stop too. If they do not, the parser fix did not cover them.
